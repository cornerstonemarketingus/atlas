# Cumulative execution allowance milestone

Issue #338 adds conservative durable admission to BusinessService.launch, using
the existing per-child scheduler BudgetLedger for execution enforcement. It does
not add another executor, global scheduler or spending authority.

The allowance is reserved before mission creation under an immediate SQLite
transaction; prevalidation rejects invalid repository/model input before reserving.
Mission IDs are generated by BusinessService and supplied to MissionService.
Mission creation exceptions are uncertain even if they look like ordinary errors:
the mission store may already have committed and started an executor. Their
reservations remain held. Observed usage is read from the existing durable
MissionService child usage; it is deliberately separate from admission holds.

The six new boundary regressions cover repeated completed missions, four real
worker connections racing for one allowance, a lost creation acknowledgement,
closing/reopening both actual mission and business SQLite stores, fail-closed
legacy records, and bounded ledger exhaustion. Existing business HTTP/auth and
dependency evidence tests continue to cover reachability through owner routes.

Acceptance limitations: reservations remain held for life, including unused
capacity, because retryable terminal lanes can execute again. Older business
history is explicitly unknown and requires a newly authorized business record
for new work. No provider billing receipts, external cash accounting, untracked
direct mission launches, migrations, reconciliation or refunds are claimed.
